Software Engineer – Backend Platform

We are looking for a backend software engineer to join our platform team.

Required:

- Experience building and maintaining REST APIs (Flask, FastAPI, or Django)
- Solid understanding of SQL databases (PostgreSQL preferred)
- Experience with containerization using Docker
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or similar)

Preferred:
- Experience with Kubernetes or other container orchestration platforms
- Cloud experience (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Experience with message queues (RabbitMQ, Kafka, or SQS)
- Knowledge of distributed systems design patterns

About the role:
You will design, build, and maintain the APIs and infrastructure that power our core product.
You will work closely with the product team to deliver new features and ensure reliability.
Strong communication skills and ability to work in a cross-functional team are important.
